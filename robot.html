Autonomous Desktop Cleaning Robot

A team-designed autonomous robot developed to clean a 4 × 4 ft workspace by combining edge detection, a rice-collection system, touchless activation, and automated completion feedback.

Engineering Design Boston University Group Project

Final prototype of the autonomous desktop cleaning robot.

OVERVIEW

This project involved designing and building an autonomous robot capable of cleaning a shared engineering workspace. The system was designed to navigate the surface, detect its boundaries, collect grains of rice, and provide a touchless start and completion signal.

The project required integrating mechanical design, electronics, sensing, programming, and physical prototyping into a single functioning system.

MY ROLE

As part of the project team, I primarily worked on the sensing system and enclosure design, contributing to the integration of the robot's electronic components into the physical prototype.

  • Integrated and tested sensors for detecting the robot's environment and supporting autonomous operation.
  • Designed the enclosure and component layout to organize and protect the electronics.
  • Iterated on physical prototypes to improve accessibility, fit, and integration between the electronic and mechanical systems.
DESIGN & ENGINEERING

The robot was developed as a combination of several integrated subsystems. Sensors were used to provide information about the robot's surroundings, while the mechanical enclosure housed the electronics and supported the cleaning mechanism.

I worked through multiple iterations of the physical design, considering component placement, wiring, accessibility, and the interaction between the enclosure and the sensing hardware.
